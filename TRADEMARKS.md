## Permitted without asking

You may use the Brand Assets, unmodified, for:

- Downloading, building and using the official Lextide application on your own
  devices, including builds that embed the official icon.
- Personal study and research of the code and user interface.
- Showing the unmodified official logo or official screenshots in non-commercial
  educational material: tutorials, course notes, project introductions, blog
  posts and study notes.
- Linking to the official project repository.
- Factual, descriptive references to the project and its author.

When you use the Brand Assets under this section, please attribute them as:


permits (such as fair use or fair dealing), do not require prior permission.
Nothing in this policy is intended to restrict those uses.

- Selling merchandise that carries the Brand Assets.
- Using the Brand Assets in commercial branding or packaging of another product.
- Commercial advertising or standalone commercial promotion built on the marks.
- Modifying the Brand Assets and presenting the result as your own brand.
- Sublicensing the Brand Assets to another commercial party.
- Using the marks in a derivative project in a way that suggests official

Attribution alone does not replace permission for these uses.
